# Book-Sales-Data-Analytics-Project

This project is an end-to-end book sales analysis using SQL and Tableau. Since reading has always been a passion of mine, I chose this dataset to better understand how books perform beyond just ratings and popularity. I wanted to look at it from a data perspective and see how genre, pricing, and customer perception actually connect to sales outcomes.

The analysis focuses on how these factors influence revenue and performance. The dashboards highlight patterns such as fiction generating the most revenue, higher ratings not always leading to higher sales, and lower-priced books driving the majority of total revenue.

Dataset: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/thedevastator/books-sales-and-ratings


## Objectives

1. Analyze sales performance across different genres.
2. Identify top-performing books based on revenue.
3. Evaluate how book ratings impact sales outcomes.
4. Examine the relationship between pricing and sales performance.
5. Compare revenue contribution across pricing tiers.
6. Identify patterns between price, demand, and overall revenue.


## Tools & Skills Used  

**SQL (MySQL)**  
* Aggregations: SUM, AVG, COUNT  
* Conditional logic using CASE WHEN  
* Grouping and filtering for business analysis  
* Derived metrics for pricing tiers and performance comparison  

**Tableau**  
* Interactive dashboard design  
* Data visualization (bar charts, scatter plots, heatmaps)  
* KPI indicators and performance breakdowns  
* Visual storytelling and layout design  


## Key Metrics & Insights  

* Total Revenue by Genre  
* Average Sales per Book  
* Top Performing Books by Revenue  
* Sales Distribution by Genre  
* Rating vs Sales Performance  
* Revenue Contribution by Pricing Tier  
* Average Sales by Price Tier
